Rules

A deck is shuffled from the cards you own. Play one card per turn, then draw. Each card has a cost paid in work force: permanent work force on its board refreshes every turn, single-use work force in your hand is consumed when spent. You may also place one permanent work force card per turn. Stack a card on another that shares a type or subtype with a strictly lower degree. Item cards change a board card's robustness: permanent items attach to it, single-use items can only be used to destroy it. A card whose robustness drops to 0 or below is destroyed, along with every card stacked on it. Items can likewise lower or raise the budget of permanent work force cards, destroying them at 0 or below. Some cards have effects that switch on when another card in the same stack (or on the work force board) has a matching name. Cards in your hand check the whole board instead. Cards with an active effect are outlined in gold. Your score is the total reach on the board. Online, players alternate turns for 10 rounds; items can also target your opponent's cards.
