Rules

Build the strongest physic's model to defeat your opponent!

Physic's cards are separated into several big branches: particle physics, gravitation, electromagnetism, condensed matter, astrophysics and thermodynamics. Each physics card has both a reach and a robustness stat, that can be viewed as attack and defense stats. The goal of the game is to play physics cards in such a way as to maximize your total reach score. At the end of the game, the player with the highest reach score is the winner.

A player can play one physics card per turn by simply placing it on their board. Physics cards however also have a cost stat, indicated on the top right corner of the card, and representing the amount of workforce required to play it. This cost is to be paid with the workforce cards. These card's purpose is solely to provide the ressource required to play physics cards and exist in two types: permanent and non-permanent. Non-permanent workforce cards are to be kept in the player's hand untill they need them to play a physics ard. When that happens, the workforce card is simply discarded out of the player's hand. For a physics card to be played, one can use one or several non-permanent physics cards such that their total workforce stat covers the cost of the card. Permanent workforce cards have a colored border and need to be placed in the workforce bench to come into play. Once placed on their bench, they stay active permanently and thus provide a permanent source of worforce that can be used to play physics card. When a permanent workforce card is used to cover the cost of a physics card, it stays on its bench and can be used again as many times as wished.

A further category of cards, the item & events cards can be used to attack your opponent's physics cards or workforce and thus weaken its game, or to protect yours. Each has its specific effect, which can be divided into two main categories: robustness and budget. The robustness is the equivalence of a defence stat for physics cards. Each physics card has its own robustness stat which can be altered by the use of item & event cards, either to decrease or to increase it. When a cards robustness drops to 0 or below, it is discarded and its reach score is thus lost. Similarly, some permanent workforce cards have a budget stat, that can be modified by specific item & events cards. When a workforce's budget drops to 0 or below, it is discarded.

Finally, physics cards can be played directly on the player's board (if the player possesses enough workforce) but also on top of another, already placed physics card. For one physics card to be allowed to be stacked on top of another, they must shard a common branch, represented by the logos on their left strip, and the top card must have a stricly lower degree stat, indicated on the top left corner. Piling cards on top of another triggers bonuses when certain cards share the same stack and enables to save space on the board for other physic cards. However, when a physics card is discarded, due to its robustness dropping to 0, all the cards that are stacked onto it are also destroyed. One shall therefore try to put their most robust cards at the bottom of a stack. Note also that once a card is played, it cannot be moved anymore, except if it is destroy. One should thus be careful when playing cards with a low degree or a weak robustness directly on the board.

A game unfolds as follows: each player starts with a hand of 5 cards. At each turn, a player can put a permanent workforce on the workforce bench and play another card (either a physics card or an itam & event card) or draw a card. After 10 turns, the game ends and the player with the highest total reach score wins.
